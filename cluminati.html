How it works

  1. Register your team. One person fills in the form as team leader. Teams can have 1 to 3 players.
  2. Join our Discord. Updates and the start details are shared there.
  3. Play from 11 December, 8 pm. You get 48 hours. Follow the clues, break the ciphers, find the flags.
