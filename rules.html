Cluminati rules

Short and plain. By registering, your team agrees to these.

  • Who can play

    Students under 18, in teams of 1 to 3 players.

  • When

    Online, from 11 to 13 December, 8 pm to 8 pm (48 hours). IST

  • AI is allowed

    You can use AI tools and any other resource you like. Searching, reading and asking tools for help are all fair. You still need to understand your answers.

  • Play fair

    Do your own team's work. Do not share clues, answers or flags with other teams, and do not use more than one team's account.

  • Stay in bounds

    Only attack what the hunt gives you. Do not attack the Cluminati website, the Discord, or anyone's accounts or devices.

  • Be kind

    No bullying, hate or spam in our Discord or anywhere else around the event.

  • Organisers decide

    If something is unclear or breaks, the Cyanet team makes the call. Break a rule and your team may lose points or be removed.
